# Business Context (Italian) — Original Blueprint

This directory contains the original Italian-language business blueprint that informed the design and product decisions for this project.

These documents were written during the pre-development planning phase and capture:

- Market positioning, pricing strategy, and roadmap (`01_STRATEGIA/`)
- Brand naming research (`02_NOMI_E_BRAND/`)
- Backend and AI architecture prompts used during development (`03_CODEX_BACKEND_PROMPTS/`)
- Frontend design prompts and references (`04_CODEX_FRONTEND_PROMPTS/`)
- GDPR/legal templates (DPA, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Policy) (`05_LEGAL_GDPR/`)
- Go-to-market scripts and sales materials (`06_GTM_E_VENDITA/`)
- Infrastructure guides and environment variable templates (`07_INFRASTRUCTURE/`)
- Launch checklist (`08_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`)
- Master overview document (`00_README_START_HERE.md`)

## Language

All documents in this directory are written in **Italian**. They are included as-is to preserve the full context behind product and architecture decisions. No automated translation is provided.

## Usage for Forks

If you are forking this project for your own use, these documents provide useful context for:

- Understanding why certain features exist and how they are scoped
- Reading the original GDPR/legal framework the codebase was designed around
- Adapting the pricing and go-to-market approach to your market

The legal templates under `05_LEGAL_GDPR/` are starting points only and are **not legal advice**. Review them with qualified legal counsel before deployment.
